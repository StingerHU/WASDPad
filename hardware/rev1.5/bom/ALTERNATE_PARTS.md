# WASDPad Revision 1.5
## Approved Alternate Parts

Document Version: 0.1

Status: Draft

This document lists approved replacement components for the Revision 1.5 hardware.

An alternate component may only be added after electrical, mechanical and functional compatibility has been verified.

---

# Approval Criteria

An alternate component shall meet the following requirements:

- Equal or better electrical ratings
- Compatible footprint
- Compatible pinout
- Compatible mechanical dimensions
- No negative effect on joystick compatibility
- No measurable degradation of latency or timing
- No reduction in protection level
- Reliable availability from recognized suppliers

# Restrictions

Parts shall not be considered interchangeable solely because they share:

- The same generic component name
- A similar package
- A similar electrical rating

Pinout, footprint, tolerances and mechanical dimensions must also be checked.
