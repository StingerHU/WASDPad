## Priority review — E-Switch 100 series

The manufacturer [RoHS declaration](https://configured-product-images.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Compliance_Safety_ConflictMaterials/Compliance/RoHS/_RoHS_Blanket.pdf) identifies the **100 series** as RoHS compliant **with exemption 6(c)** (lead in copper alloy). The [REACH 253 declaration](https://configured-product-images.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Compliance_Safety_ConflictMaterials/Compliance/REACH/_REACH_Blanket.pdf) reports **lead CAS 7439-92-1 above 0.1%** for the same series. These statements do **not** automatically mean the product may not be sold, but they require appropriate Article 33 supply-chain communication and checking exemption scope/validity where relevant. **Do not report these switches as SVHC-free.**

- Retrieve the latest **YAGEO RC and MLCC REACH certificates**; URLs indexed by public search for SVHC (251) did not reliably resolve in live lookup. Ensure certificate manufacturing-plant coverage and current candidate-list version.
- Save dated copies of declarations at the date of production purchase. **RoHS exemption** and **REACH SVHC disclosure** are different regulatory matters.
- `PCB FEATURE / DNP` is not a purchased component; obtain PCB laminate, solder mask, surface finish, and fabrication chemical declarations separately from PCB manufacturer, if required.

## Link provenance / grading

**Exact MPN status:** manufacturer page identifies the part and explicitly states RoHS/REACH.  
**Series blanket declaration:** signed/manufacturer-issued document may apply across multiple part numbers; verify family and valid revision.  
**Manufacturer portal:** there are separate documents available for manual download (e.g., Samsung MLCC); the portal itself is not the declaration.  
**Distributor listing:** informational only; request the primary declaration for compliance sign-off.  
**Provisional source:** manufacturer PDF was indexed by search but could not be opened in the live check; do not claim verified until downloaded.

**Distribution:** This public, component-level link register contains no KiCad design files, production Gerbers, or confidential fabrication datasets. The final manufacturing files are maintained privately for Commodore.
